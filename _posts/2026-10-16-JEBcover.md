We are delighted to see a photograph by René Warren featured on the cover of the October 2026 issue of the *Journal of Evolutionary Biology* ([Volume 39, Issue 10](https://academic.oup.com/jeb/issue/39/10)).

[![October 2026 cover of the Journal of Evolutionary Biology, featuring a hummingbird approaching red flowers. Photograph by René L. Warren.]( /assets/gallery/2026-JEBcover-october.jpeg)](https://academic.oup.com/jeb/issue/39/10)

The cover reflects the broader biological diversity that comparative genomics seeks to understand. Our research develops computationally efficient approaches such as **ntSynt** for comparing genomes across species, with **ntSynt-viz** providing interactive visualizations of the resulting synteny patterns.

[Explore hummingbird genome synteny!](https://www.birollab.ca/viz/Trochilidae_hummingbirds_ribbon-plot_tree.html)

Read our [ntSynt-viz paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/jeb/voag079) in the *Journal of Evolutionary Biology*.

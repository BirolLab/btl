We are delighted to see a [photograph by René Warren](https://warrenlr.github.io) featured on the cover of the October 2026 issue of the *Journal of Evolutionary Biology* ([Volume 39, Issue 10](https://academic.oup.com/jeb/issue/39/10)).

The photograph highlights the diversity of life that comparative genomics helps us study. Our research develops computationally efficient approaches such as **ntSynt** for comparing genomes across species, with **ntSynt-viz** providing interactive visualizations of the resulting synteny patterns.

Read our [ntSynt paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-025-02455-w) in *BMC Biology*

Read our [ntSynt-viz paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/jeb/voag079) in the *Journal of Evolutionary Biology*

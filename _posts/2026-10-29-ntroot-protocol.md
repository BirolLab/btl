We are pleased to announce the publication of our latest manuscript, [*ntRoot: Genetic Ancestry Inference Across Diverse Genomic Data Types*](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpz1.70491), in *Current Protocols*.

This article provides step-by-step protocols for using ntRoot, our scalable genetic ancestry inference tool, across a variety of genomic data types, including whole-genome sequencing, whole-exome sequencing, genome assemblies, SNP arrays, and variant call files. With support for both global and local ancestry inference, ntRoot offers a flexible and efficient approach for characterizing genetic ancestry in population-scale genomic studies.

ntRoot is freely available on [GitHub](https://github.com/BirolLab/ntRoot).
